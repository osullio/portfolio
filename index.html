About

I'm a Computer & Electrical Engineering student at Trinity College Dublin from Kildare. I've always been drawn to coding and mathematics, from reaching a regional maths final in school. My recent modules include computer vision, digital signal processing and deep learning.

I've done two internships. At Cadence Design Systems I spent eight months on the VMT Infra team, prototyping Qt user interfaces for Virtuoso and refactoring core C++ code. Before that I spent four months at Happystack, a startup, where I worked across engineering, marketing and finance. There I wrote a Python tool that used the OpenAI API to analyse and organise data, and led a research project on Generative Engine Optimization.

Away from the desk I play Gaelic football and hurling. I won an intermediate county football medal in 2024 and reached the senior hurling county final in 2023. I also play traditional Irish music and placed third at the Kildare Fleadh Cheoil.
